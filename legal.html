Privacy Policy

What we access: basic profile information of our own TikTok account (to confirm we post to the right account) and permission to upload and publish videos to that account.

What we store: only the access token for our own account, kept on our own computer. We do not collect, store, sell or share data about any other TikTok user.

Third parties: videos are sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Deletion: access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings, which invalidates the stored token.
